Controls

ctrl + mouse-drag or tap the button and drag with one finger: Selecting a single activity shows its details. With a mouse, shift-drag zooms to a box.

alt + mouse-drag or two-finger gestures: 3D Rotate and Pitch

Live URL

The map view and the animation settings are kept in the page's URL, so anyone you share it with sees the same view.

Video Export

The button records the animation as an MP4: tap it, size the box over the part of the map you want, and tap again to record. One full loop of the animation is recorded, so the video repeats seamlessly; a loop longer than 30 seconds (the longest video Strava accepts) is cut off at 30.
