Controls

To select activities, drag a box over them: hold ctrl and drag with a mouse, or tap the button and drag with one finger. Selecting a single activity shows its details. With a mouse, shift-drag zooms to a box.

The map view and the animation settings are kept in the page's URL, so anyone you share it with sees the same view.

Video Export

The button records the animation as an MP4: tap it, size the box over the part of the map you want, and tap again to record. One full loop of the animation is recorded, so the video repeats seamlessly; a loop longer than 20 seconds is cut off at 20.
